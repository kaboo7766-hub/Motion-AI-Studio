# Motion AI Studio — 2D Bone & Pin Character Rigging (No AI Credits Needed)

## New Interactive Part-by-Part Character Animation
- **Click-to-Place Motion Pins:** Choose any body part (**Head, Mouth, Right Hand, Left Hand, Chest, Right Leg, Left Leg, or Anchor Pin**) and click directly on that spot on the uploaded image.
- **Drag & Fine-Tune:** Drag any placed pin on the canvas to adjust its exact position and see its influence radius.
- **Anchor Pins (`مسمار تثبيت`):** Lock specific areas of the character or background so only the selected limbs/mouth/head move cleanly without warping the rest of the image.
- **Real-Time Preview & Video Export:** Works 100% locally in the browser with zero cost or external API keys.
